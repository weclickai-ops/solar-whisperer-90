Technology

How the tracker follows the sun.

A fixed-tilt array remains in a single position and reaches peak output only briefly at solar noon. A tracker maintains a productive angle from morning through afternoon — provided its orientation remains accurate. That accuracy is determined by the controller, not by the structure.

How it works

The control sequence.

01

Calculate

Astronomical algorithms determine solar position for the site.

02

Track

The controller derives the tracker angle required.

03

Move

A single-point linear actuator turns the torque tube.

04

Optimise

Backtracking removes row-to-row shading at low sun angles.

05

Protect

Flat stow in high wind; nighttime stow on schedule.

Why it matters

Where the additional yield comes from.

The shaded area represents generation a fixed-tilt array does not capture. Over the operating life of a plant, this amounts to 15–25% more energy from the same modules.

Terrain and safety

Built for imperfect ground.

Trackers install along undulating sites without regrading, and return to flat stow when wind conditions or nightfall require it.
